# PHASE 3D-B — Novelty red team (final scientific round)

Primary evidence is frozen at `icassp-10of10-phase3d-b-primary-transfer-frozen` (`4fd880b`). Coefficient transfer is a suite-wide strong failure (66/614; task-macro median 0.0476; 20/20 FRAGILE). Response transfer is mixed-to-strong (585/614; task-macro median 1.0; 15/20 ≥95%; 1/20 <75%).

Phase 3C already showed Type-I probes were *not* a clean holdout. | Low | A catalog-excluded, specification-certified, prospectively generated challenge is not a random split of the fitting universe. | `CLOSED` |

No high-severity attack remains `OPEN` except scoped limitations (finite generator palette; incomplete invalid coverage; representation-specific coefficient failure). Those are named, not hidden.

Strongest remaining attack: D2+D5 together — a reviewer may still say “of course new Remez taps need new coefficient prototypes.” The manuscript-safe reply is the *joint* pattern: ambient centers exist, response catalogs often transfer, published libraries do not, and the same generators are ordinary DSP designs.
