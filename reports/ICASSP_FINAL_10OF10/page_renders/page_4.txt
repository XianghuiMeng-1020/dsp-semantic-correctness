Response distance is the stronger reference oracle of the
two, as classical approximation already suggests: indepen-
dently verified valids can sit close to hr on constrained bands
while remaining far in coefficient space. It is still not exact
on this universe. The single positive response gap (one tight
IIR high-pass instance, order 10−6) is a numerical near-tie,
not evidence that |H| RMSE recovers St in general.
Pooled FRR remains source-dependent: 58/88 library,
14/24 first-principles, and 298/300 random-valid occupants
exceed τR after independent verification. That is why the
primary unit is the task, and why the confirmatory claims are
the 20/20 gap, inversion, same-order, and reference-choice
counts rather than 370/412.
The study is restricted to one mask family. It is not a
claim about beams, adaptive filters, filter banks, or DSP eval-
uation in general. Requiring Type-I linear phase or a declared
maximum order as part of St would define a different feasi-
ble set. The independent verifier does not certify the contin-
uous mask. IIR same-order evidence uses library occupants
rather than a pole/zero feasible-set program. The generated-
code arm is a witness on four original masks and is not pooled
with the constructed n.
Canonicalization is part of the claim, not a preprocessing
footnote. After a0 = 1, Type-I endpoint preservation, and
exclusion of zero-padding, sign-flip, and pure-scale encod-
ings, the same-order Type-I alternatives remain distinct trans-
fer functions. The confirmatory distance is therefore not an
artifact of comparing unequal truncated coefficient vectors.
A practical reading of Proposition 1 is operational. If a
test bench keeps a single golden Hamming or Butterworth de-
sign and a scalar coefficient (or band-RMSE) threshold, com-
pute DV and DI on the independently labeled universe it ac-
tually scores. A negative gap means that no retuning of that
threshold can make the reference test exact for that specifica-
tion. The remedy is to score St, or to enlarge the specification
until the intended realization is effectively singleton.
Tightening the mask did not restore exactness.
The
Type-I probe still returned independently verified same-order,
reference-discordant valids on all eight tight FIR instances,
and coefficient Gr remained negative on all four tight IIR
instances. A tighter contract shrinks Vt; it does not, by itself,
make Aτ,r coincide with that smaller set.
Finite K has the same geometry. The K-oracle replaces
one ball by a union of balls around library occupants. On
this universe that union still fails to cover the independently
verified valids without also covering a nearer invalid, for K =
1, 3, 5 and for the full library set. Covering Vt ∩Ut would
require either the specification predicate or a reference set that
is no longer a small canonical catalog.
The four label flips are a protocol result, not a bookkeep-
ing detail. The 4096-point construction checker accepted four
tight firwin2 library occupants that the independent veri-
fier rejects at the band edge after Nf = 131072 and local ex-
tremum refinement. Those occupants are excluded from the
valid set. Using the construction checker as the final labeler
would have kept the older 416 count and would have scored
a membership oracle against the same predicate that defined
the labels. That circularity is why the authoritative counts are
412 valids and four recorded flips.
The same-order FIR probe is not a search for a convenient
counterexample. Directions were frozen (seed 20260826) be-
fore disagreement was inspected: basis, frozen-seed random,
tap combinations, and predetermined same-order library dif-
ferences. A Type-I linear program on 512 constraint frequen-
cies per band, with a predetermined interior margin and line-
search backoff, produced independently verified alternatives
on 16/16 FIR tasks. IIR evidence is weaker by construc-
tion: it uses existing same-order library occupants rather than
a pole/zero program. Even under that restriction the four IIR
tasks remain reference-discordant.
Suite S is the complementary control, not a second leader-
board. On those eight unique-map identities the canonical
occupant is effectively the specification in the chosen rep-
resentation, Gr > 0 on 8/8 tasks, and a reference-distance
test is exact on the evaluated universe. The contrast is the
claim: reference matching is the right oracle when St is ef-
fectively singleton, and it is not an exact oracle when St is
a non-unique mask on this universe. A finite catalog of li-
brary references remains a catalog of realizations. It does not
become Vt by taking a min-distance union; K equal to the
full independently verified library set still fails exact recov-
ery (0/20). The evaluated invalids include 160/160 indepen-
dently verified near-boundary mutants, so the nearest-invalid
side of Gr is not an easy far-field mutant. What Gr ≤0 li-
censes is narrower than a global impossibility theorem. It is a
certificate on the independently labeled Ut actually scored: no
scalar threshold on the chosen d recovers St there. It does not
prove that every DSP mask is non-separable, that every gen-
erated implementation is valid, or that a different distance or
a different specification predicate would fail. The task-cluster
bootstrap CI [0.871, 0.925] is likewise a statement about these
20 tasks, not about DSP evaluation in general. The four flips
sit on tight firwin2 band edges; they are not random-valid
occupants and they are not recycled as mechanism invalids.
6. CONCLUSION
When a DSP specification defines a feasible set, a scalar
reference-distance test is an exact correctness oracle on an
evaluated universe if and only if the reference-separability
gap is positive. On the independently verified Suite N uni-
verse that gap is non-positive for coefficient distance on every
task, every library reference, and every tested finite K; same-
order feasible-set probing does not remove the mismatch.
Reference matching remains the right test for effectively
singleton specifications.
