Proposition 1 (finite-universe exactness). On a finite Ut, a
scalar threshold on d(·, hr) classifies every evaluated valid
and invalid exactly iff DV (r) ≤τ < DI(r) for some τ.

is evaluation geometry; it does not certify extrema over all
filters.

3. INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION AND
PROTOCOL
Construction uses a 4096-point freqz search checker. That
checker is computationally convenient and is not the final
labeler.
Final VALID/INVALID status is assigned by an
independent verifier that does not call the search checker:
Nf = 131072 magnitude samples, local extremum refine-
ment on each constrained band, second-order-section evalua-
tion for IIR, a0 = 1 normalization, and the registered residual
floors (FIR 10−6, IIR 10−3) and pole-radius bound 0.999.
Dense sampling is a numerical certificate, not a continuous-
frequency proof. Four previously admitted library firwin2
occupants on tight low-pass and high-pass masks failed the
independent verifier at a constrained-band edge (normalized
residual up to 1.6 × 10−2) and were relabeled invalid. The
construction checker had reported a zero residual on the same
filters, which is why final labels cannot remain self-certified
by the 4096-point search.
Two suites are scored (Table 1). Suite S comprises eight
singleton identities (integer delay, circular and linear convo-
lution conventions, lag-0 energy, alias fold, frequency rescale,
Nyquist frequency, and δ[n −D]). Suite N comprises 20
instances from one magnitude-mask family: FIR low-pass,
high-pass, band-pass, and band-stop at fs = 8 and 16 kHz,
each loose and tight (16 FIR), plus IIR low-pass and high-
pass at 8 kHz (4 IIR). Tight masks are mechanical (pass rip-
ple and stop ceiling ×1/5; stop edge moved halfway toward
the facing pass). Canonical hr is the shortest-odd Hamming
firwin (FIR) or the lowest-order butter (IIR) that meets
the contract.
Suite N candidates include classical library designers,
first-principles FIR windowed-sinc and frequency sampling
(numpy only) [5, 7], and random-valid search that admits on
the search checker only. After independent verification the
valid count is 412 (88 library, 24 first-principles, 300 random-
valid). Invalids are 144 mechanism mutants plus a second,
predetermined near-boundary set (pass-drop and stop-lift
at fixed ε ∈{0.002, 0.005, 0.010, 0.020}), independently
verified invalid (160/160 boundary mutants). The primary
statistical unit is the specification.
