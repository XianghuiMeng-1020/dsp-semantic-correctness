5. DISCUSSION
A reference-distance oracle answers “near this realization?”
It answers “in Vt?” only when Gr > 0 on the evaluated uni-
verse. That happens on Suite S, where the specification is
effectively singleton in the chosen representation. It does not
happen on these 20 magnitude-mask instances, for coefficient
distance or (with one near-tie) response distance, for any li-
brary choice of hr, or for a finite set of library references.
